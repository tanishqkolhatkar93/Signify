“Imagine having something important to say… but the person standing right in front of you cannot understand your language.


For millions of Deaf people, this communication barrier is part of everyday life.

Communication is a human right.


The World Health Organization estimates that 430 million people worldwide having hearing loss.

In India, government sources have cited an estimate of approximately 63 million people with significant hearing impairment.

So we asked one question: What if technology could help bridge that communication gap?”

“I build Signify — our AI-powered sign-language communication platform.

Our goal is simple: help Deaf and hearing people communicate more easily, with less dependence on someone else to interpret every interaction.”

Let me show you how it works.

First, Signify makes its supported vocabulary visible. We’re demonstrating Indian Sign Language.

Before enabling the camera, the application asks for consent.

Now, when I perform a supported sign, computer vision extracts hand landmarks. Our recognition logic evaluates those landmarks and checks whether the result is reliable enough to use.

Watch what happens next.

The recognized meaning appears as text, and Signify can speak the corresponding phrase in English and Hindi.”


demo 2-  Now lets see Speech to text and supported signs

“Communication cannot be a one-way street.

Now let’s reverse the interaction.

A hearing person speaks. Signify converts that speech into text, 

When the phrase matches our supported vocabulary, the application can display the corresponding verified ISL sign media.

This creates a practical, two-way communication workflow, while making the boundary between supported and unsupported phrases clear.”

demo 3 - Technical innovation 

What makes Signify technically meaningful is how we combine these capabilities into one System.

Our React and TypeScript frontend handles the user experience. MediaPipe extracts hand landmarks, our gesture classifier identifies supported signs, and a Python FastAPI backend coordinates processing and language-service integrations.

A bounded orchestration layer routes tasks, validates results, and handles uncertain predictions.

We are not simply adding a chatbot to a camera. We are building a system with defined actions, testable decisions, and explicit failure handling.”



demo 4 - Now lets talk about Trust and Safety .

“In accessibility technology, an incorrect answer can be worse than no answer.

That’s why Signify includes an explicit unrecognized-sign state, makes its supported vocabulary visible, and asks for consent before recording or using a person’s camera input.

We believe responsible AI starts with being transparent about what a system can—and cannot—do.
